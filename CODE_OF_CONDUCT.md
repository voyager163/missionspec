# Code of Conduct

MissionSpec welcomes participation from people with different backgrounds,
experience, and viewpoints. This code applies to project issues, pull requests,
reviews, and other spaces managed for the project, including conduct reports.
It also applies when someone is explicitly representing the project elsewhere.

## Working together

Discuss the work with care for the people doing it. Explain the reasoning behind
a suggestion, ask before assuming intent, and make room for questions and
corrections. Disagreement about an implementation or project direction is
normal; criticism should address the proposal rather than attack the person.

Respect a request to stop an unwanted interaction. Do not harass, threaten,
intimidate, or demean people; use discriminatory abuse; direct unwanted sexual
attention at participants; impersonate others; or reveal someone's private
information without permission. Repeatedly disrupting discussions or retaliating
against someone for a good-faith report is not acceptable.

## Reporting a conduct concern

Email **ask.msncontrol@gmail.com** for conduct concerns. This is the authorized
public conduct contact, not a technical support or security-reporting address.
Use [Issues](https://github.com/voyager163/missionspec/issues) for ordinary public
support and [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) for the current security-reporting policy.

Describe what happened, where it happened, and any immediate concern. Include
only the information needed to understand the incident, such as links to public
project interactions. Do not send credentials, private source, `.env` files, or
raw diagnostic archives. You do not need to confront the person involved or
make a public accusation before reporting.

The project currently has one maintainer, who handles reports. The maintainer
will seek to limit sharing to what is needed to assess and address the concern,
but cannot promise absolute confidentiality or a response within a particular
time. Consider those limits before sending sensitive personal information.

## Handling concerns

The maintainer may ask for clarification, request a change in behavior, edit or
remove project content, pause an interaction, or restrict participation
temporarily or permanently. The response should consider the impact, context,
severity, and any repeated behavior. Serious harm can justify an immediate
restriction without a sequence of warnings.

Participants, including the maintainer, are expected to follow these standards.
If you think a decision missed important context, reply through the same conduct
contact with that context and the outcome you are requesting. The project does
not promise an independent review body or a particular outcome, and may decline
to handle matters outside its scope.
